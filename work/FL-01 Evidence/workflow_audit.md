## Purpose

This workflow audit maps recurring tasks from my week across my FlyRank internship, technical learning, and Amazon KDP publishing work. The goal is to identify where I should work independently, where AI can assist with review, where I should collaborate with AI, and where automation may be appropriate.

## 2. Three Target Tasks for FL-02 Through FL-04

### Target Task 1 — Completing FlyRank Internship Assignments

**Task:** Understand assignment requirements, break them into actionable steps, complete the required work, and prepare the final submission.

**Done well means:**
- Every stated assignment requirement is identified.
- The work is broken into clear, actionable steps.
- All required sections, code, and evidence are completed.
- The final submission satisfies the assignment requirements.
- I understand and can explain the work I submit.
- AI assists my workflow without making decisions that I should make myself.

### Target Task 2 — Debugging Python/Jupyter Notebook Errors
**Task:** Diagnose and fix errors in Python/Jupyter notebook code used for assignments and ML work.

**Done well means:**
- The notebook runs from beginning to end without errors.
- The underlying cause of each error is identified.
- The solution fixes the actual problem rather than simply hiding or bypassing it.
- I understand what was changed and why.
- The resulting output is consistent with the intended analysis.
- I can explain the relevant fix without relying entirely on AI.

### Target Task 3 — Writing and Editing KDP Book Content

**Task:** Develop, review, and improve nonfiction book content for Amazon KDP.

**Done well means:**
- The content follows the required book structure and formatting.
- Information is relevant to the book's topic and intended reader.
- Important factual claims are checked before publication.
- Sections do not unnecessarily repeat information.
- The content meets the required word-count targets.
- The final writing is clear, useful, and publication-ready after human review.
